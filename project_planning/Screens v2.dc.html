Screens, second pass

Rebuilt against your six notes. New palette and type, status colors from the Okabe–Ito colorblind-safe set with a glyph on every badge, fewer panels and bigger gutters, everything one step larger, one obvious focal point per screen, the device drawer made unmistakable, and the night theme finished.

What changed

Your six notes, answered

Palette & fonts

Warm paper neutrals instead of cool grey, taking the cue from the forest photography and editorial rhythm on your site. Headings are Source Serif 4; UI and tables are IBM Plex Sans; identifiers stay mono. All three vendored as woff2 in the repo, so §15.1 air-gapped hosts still render correctly.

Buttons are ink, not color. Primary actions are near-black. That hands the whole color budget to status, so nothing on screen competes with a firing alert.

Colorblind safety

The old red/green pair was the real failure. Statuses now come from Okabe–Ito, ordered by lightness so the set also reads as a ramp in greyscale, and every badge carries a shape-distinct glyph and a text label. Color is a third channel, never the only one.

Map markers encode level as shape, status as glyph, and alerts as a badge with its own outline. A deuteranopic operator loses nothing.

Clutter, size, focus

Body type went 13 → 15px, grid rows 28 → 36px, nothing renders below 12px. The dashboard dropped from four metric tiles and two side panels to one hero number and two panels, and the pod table lost three columns.

Each screen now has exactly one thing at display size. On the dashboard it is the online count; everywhere else it is the page title. Eyebrow labels above sections give the eye a path.

In v1 the drawer was a white column with a hairline border sitting flush against a white map panel. Nothing said "this opened", nothing said "this belongs to that marker", and nothing said how to close it. Four fixes, all visible below.

01The map dims behind it and the selected marker keeps a halo, so the drawer clearly belongs to one device.

03A real deep shadow plus a 3px accent edge lifts it off the map instead of butting against it.
04A titled header with an explicit Close button and an Esc hint.
